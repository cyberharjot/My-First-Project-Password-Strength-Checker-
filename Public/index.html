In today's digital landscape, cyber threats are more prevalent than ever, yet many individuals continue to use weak or easily guessable passwords. This significantly increases the risk of unauthorized access and data breaches.

This project demonstrates the importance of using strong passwords:

  • Brute Force Simulation – Shows how easy it is to crack weak passwords.
  • Password Strength Checker – Lets you check how strong your password is.
  • Password Generator – Helps you generate secure, strong passwords.
